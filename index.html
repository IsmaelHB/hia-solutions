What the agent does

A complete receptionist,
not just a chatbot.

01
Answers inbound calls

Your agent picks up in under 3 seconds, introduces itself with your practice name, and opens a natural conversation.

02
Qualifies each request

It identifies the reason for the call — new patient, urgent, follow-up, question — and routes to the right action without human effort.

03
Books appointments

It checks real-time availability and creates the appointment directly in your calendar. No double entry, no delay.

04
Reschedules & cancels

Patients can call back to move or cancel. The agent finds their booking and updates the calendar automatically.

05
Handles FAQ

Hours, address, rates, which practitioner to see — the agent knows your practice inside out and answers without bothering you.

06
Transfers when needed

For complex situations or real emergencies, the agent transfers the call to the right person instantly.
